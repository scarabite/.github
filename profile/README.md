Scarabite is a data, intelligence and engineering firm. We build the systems
that turn raw data into decisions: AI and automation, data pipelines,
external intelligence, and the cloud infrastructure that holds it together.
We work with mid-market businesses that need reliable systems, not prototypes.

We stay involved after deployment, not just through it. The same engineers
who scope a project are the ones who build it, ship it, and support it long
after launch, so nothing gets lost in handoffs and nothing gets left behind
once the project goes live.
